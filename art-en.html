«Fifth Element» Exhibition Website

You have landed on the page of a project dedicated to the system of the Five Elements. Here you will find the schedule and locations of exhibitions, see the works of the author Konstantin Medragon, and get acquainted with his book «The Fifth Element · 五行».

A Universal Language of Reality

The Five Elements are a universal way of classifying reality itself — a way of describing existence through five colors, five sounds, five tastes, five emotions, and five pairs of organs. Everything that surrounds us, everything we experience, can be understood through this prism.

The exhibition presents five paintings, each corresponding to one of the Elements. The properties of each Element are conveyed not only through color but also through texture — powerful, tactile images that embody the spirit of each element.

Water — depth, mystery, and hidden threat beneath a calm surface. It is the force that carves canyons over millennia and the whisper that moves through dreams. In this painting, Water speaks through deep blues and blacks, through flowing and receding textures, inviting the viewer to look beneath the surface.

Fire — transformation, passion, and the relentless drive to consume and create anew. It is the spark of inspiration and the flame of destruction. The painting burns with reds, oranges, and golds, its textures rough and radiant, as if the canvas itself is ablaze.

Wood — growth, expansion, and the gentle persistence of life pushing through stone. It is the first stirring of spring and the slow unfolding of destiny. Greens and browns dominate this canvas, and textures rise and branch, reaching upward toward the light.

Metal — precision, clarity, and the edge of truth. It is the blade that cuts through illusion and the mirror that reflects the soul. The painting is executed in whites, silvers, and cool greys, its textures hard and brilliant, like the surface of a polished sword.

Earth — the center, the fulcrum, the place where all things return and from which all things emerge. It is the stomach of the human being — the cauldron of reflection and transformation. Yellows, ochres, and warm browns fill this canvas, and textures are dense, grounded, and endlessly fertile.
