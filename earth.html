Earth

Earth gives birth to myriads of things, and to Earth they all return. It is the element that connects cycles and creates meaning — the ground on which the human being is born. Earth is the fertile foundation of life, and also what deeply completes the cycle of creation.

Earth carries certainty and support. It bestows and rewards. It is the element of the center — the fifth element, the one that holds the other four in place. In a person, Earth corresponds to the capacity for self-determination: the ability to decide who one is, to stand on one's own ground, to become a self. This is what makes a human being human — and this is why the fifth element is placed at the center of the system.

When Earth is in balance, reflection turns into a gift: the talent of a writer, the ability to perceive concepts, to hold meaning, to give form to what has no shape. When Earth loses its balance, the same reflection becomes cyclical — thoughts that circle without arriving, the inability to make a decision. It is like a tree whose trunk is buried in soil: it cannot grow, because what should be its foundation has become its prison.

Earth is tied to the Stomach and Spleen, together with the pancreas — the organs of receptivity and assimilation, the ability to take in and transform physical food. Its planet is Saturn, the slow elder, the lord of structure. Its taste is sweet, the taste of nourishment and reward. And its color is yellow — the color that, in ancient China, only the emperor was allowed to wear.
