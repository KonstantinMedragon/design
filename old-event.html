About the Exhibition

The exhibition takes place on the second floor, in a 100-square-metre hall. Five paintings are arranged within the space in the order of the two fundamental cycles of the Five Elements — creation and control.

The evening opens with a live sound performance: five sounds, each belonging to one element, merging into a single sound landscape.
